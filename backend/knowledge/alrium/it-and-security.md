# IT and security

## Laptops and accounts

Every employee gets a company laptop on day one, with full-disk encryption and device management installed. Your account gives you email, chat, the HR portal and the tools for your role. Install software only from the self-service catalog, and ask the IT help desk for anything else. Return your laptop and accessories on your last day.

## Passwords and sign-in

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, the VPN, source code and finance tools.
- Use the company password manager. Never reuse your Alrium password anywhere else.
- Never share your password, even with IT staff. IT will never ask for it.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes.

## Phishing and suspicious email

Don't click links, open attachments or reply to suspicious emails, and don't forward them to colleagues. Use the "Report phishing" button in your mail app, or tell security@alrium.example within one hour. Be especially careful with urgent requests about payroll, bank details or gift cards: Finance never changes bank details because of an email.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to it-helpdesk@alrium.example and security@alrium.example within one hour. IT can lock and wipe the device remotely. Nobody is blamed for reporting quickly.

## Data handling

Alrium classifies information as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data is always Confidential or Restricted: keep it in approved systems, never on personal devices or in personal email. Don't paste customer data or source code into AI tools that IT and Security haven't approved.

## Personal devices

You can read company email and chat on a personal phone once it's enrolled in device management. Personal laptops can't connect to internal systems.
