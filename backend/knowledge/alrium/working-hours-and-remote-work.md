# Working hours and remote work

## Working hours

A normal working week is Monday to Friday, about 40 hours. Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST: be available for meetings and messages during that window. Outside core hours, plan your day with your team.

## Remote work

You can work remotely up to 2 days a week, with your manager's agreement. Mark your remote days in the team calendar. Fully remote work is possible by exception, approved by your department head and the People team. When you work remotely, use the VPN for internal systems.

## Working from another city or country

Working from another city in India for up to 4 weeks a year is fine with your manager's approval. Working from another country needs approval from the People team and Finance at least 30 days ahead, because of tax and data protection rules.

## Overtime and on-call

Alrium doesn't expect regular overtime. If a launch or an incident needs extra hours, take the time back within the next month, agreed with your manager. Engineers on the on-call rota get an on-call allowance of ₹1,500 a week, plus time off after night-time callouts.
