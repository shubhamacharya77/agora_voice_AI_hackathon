# Offices and facilities

## Bengaluru office (headquarters)

The headquarters is at 4th Cross, Indiranagar, Bengaluru. It's open 8:00 to 20:00, Monday to Friday. Weekend access needs a facilities request at least one working day ahead.

- Floor 1: reception, the pantry and Room 1A (6 people).
- Floor 2: Engineering, Room 2B (8 people, with video conferencing) and the IT cupboard.
- Floor 3: leadership, the Board room (14 people) and the supply room.

## Pune office

The Pune team works from a co-working floor in Baner, open 9:00 to 19:00 on weekdays. It has two bookable meeting rooms: Sahyadri (6 people) and Mula (4 people). The co-working front desk handles visitors and deliveries.

## Meeting rooms

Book rooms in the calendar, or ask CoO. Book no more than 2 hours at a time, and release rooms you no longer need. The Board room is reserved for leadership and client meetings on Monday mornings until 12:00.

## Visitors and deliveries

Register visitors with reception (facilities@alrium.example) at least one working day ahead. Visitors show photo ID, wear a visitor badge, and are escorted by their host at all times. Personal deliveries go to reception and must be collected the same day.

## Supplies, pantry and parking

- The supply room on floor 3 holds paper, markers, notebooks and chargers. Facilities checks stock every week and reorders anything below its reorder level.
- The pantry on floor 1 has tea, coffee and snacks all day. Lunch is served from 12:30 to 14:30.
- Parking has 12 car spaces and 30 two-wheeler spaces, first come, first served. Visitor parking must be booked with reception.

## Cleaning and maintenance

The office is cleaned daily from 7:00 to 8:00 and from 19:00 to 20:00. Air-conditioning maintenance happens on one Saturday each month, and Facilities emails the date in advance. Report anything broken through the help desk.

## Safety

There are fire exits at both ends of every floor. First-aid kits are at reception and on each floor, and every floor has a trained fire warden. In an emergency, leave by the stairs (not the lift) and gather at the assembly point in the front car park.
