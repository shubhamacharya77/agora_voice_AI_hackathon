# Vendors and contracts

The Operations Manager owns this register. Contract documents are in the finance drive.

## Facilities vendors

- BrightClean Services (office cleaning, Bengaluru): ₹1.8 lakh a month. The contract renews on 31 March 2027, with 60 days' notice to change or end it. The quarterly review call with the vendor is due this month.
- CoolAir Systems (air-conditioning maintenance): ₹45,000 a quarter, with one Saturday visit a month. Renews on 30 June 2027.
- SafeGuard Security (front desk and night security): ₹2.4 lakh a month. Renews on 31 December 2026, and renegotiation has started.

## Food and pantry

- FreshPlate Caterers (weekday lunch, Bengaluru): ₹180 per meal. Renews on 31 May 2027.
- Pantry supplies are bought monthly through the procurement portal. Coffee beans and printer paper are on automatic reorder.

## IT and connectivity

- SecureNet (office internet, 1 Gbps with a 200 Mbps backup line): ₹60,000 a month. Renews on 31 January 2027.
- Laptop leasing (180 devices): a 3-year lease ending in August 2027. Replacement planning starts in March 2027.

## Rules for vendors

Any new vendor costing more than ₹5 lakh a year needs three quotes and the Finance Lead's approval. Contracts need a data protection clause if the vendor will see employee or customer data. Put renewal dates in the calendar at least 90 days ahead.
