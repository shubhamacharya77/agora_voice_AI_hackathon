# Code of conduct

## Respect and inclusion

Treat everyone with respect, whatever their role, background or opinions. Alrium doesn't tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination, whether in the office, online or at work events.

## Preventing sexual harassment

Alrium follows the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act. Complaints go to the Internal Committee at ic@alrium.example and are handled confidentially. Retaliating against anyone who raises a concern is itself a breach of this code.

## Conflicts of interest and gifts

Tell your manager and the People team about anything that could conflict with your work at Alrium, such as a side business with a customer or vendor. You may accept small gifts worth up to ₹2,000. Politely decline anything bigger, and anything offered while a deal or purchase is being decided.

## Confidentiality and social media

Keep customer, employee and company information confidential, during and after your time at Alrium. On social media, speak for yourself, not for Alrium, unless the marketing team has asked you to.

## Speaking up

If you see something that breaks this code or the law, tell your manager or the People team, or write to ethics@alrium.example, which you can do anonymously. Alrium investigates every report and protects people who speak up in good faith.
