# About Alrium

Alrium Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Alrium") builds software that helps operations teams run their day-to-day work: approvals, requests, facilities and reporting. Alrium was founded in 2019 in Bengaluru and has about 180 employees across two offices and remote teams.

## Mission and values

Our mission is to make day-to-day operations effortless. We work by four values:

- Ownership: if you see a problem, you own it until it reaches the right person.
- Clarity: write things down, say what you mean, and make decisions visible.
- Care: for customers, for colleagues, and for the quality of the work.
- Craft: ship small, improve often, and leave things better than you found them.

## Products and customers

- Alrium Flow: workflow automation for approvals, requests and recurring operations tasks.
- Alrium Insight: dashboards and reports on operational data such as tickets, spend and service levels.
- CoO: a voice assistant for operations, piloted internally by the Operations team.

Our customers are mid-sized companies in logistics, retail and healthcare, mostly in India and Southeast Asia.
