# Security incident response

This runbook is for administrators and the incident response team.

## Severity levels

- SEV1: a confirmed breach of customer or employee data, ransomware, or a full outage of a customer-facing system.
- SEV2: a suspected breach, a compromised account with access to sensitive systems, or a partial outage.
- SEV3: a contained issue, such as a phishing click with no password entered, or a lost laptop that is encrypted.

## The first hour

1. Contain: disable compromised accounts, isolate affected devices and revoke exposed keys.
2. Tell the IT and Security Lead (Rohan Das) and security@alrium.example within one hour of noticing.
3. Preserve evidence: don't wipe machines or delete emails until Security says so.
4. Open an incident channel and name an incident commander. For SEV1 and SEV2, that's the IT and Security Lead.

## Communication and reporting

- The CEO and the Head of People approve all communication to staff and customers.
- Certain cyber incidents must be reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them. The IT and Security Lead decides whether this applies and files the report.
- Customers whose data is affected are told within the time set in their contracts, usually 72 hours.

## After the incident

Hold a blameless review within 5 working days. Record what happened, the timeline, the impact and the follow-up actions, each with an owner and a due date. Share a summary with leadership@alrium.example.
