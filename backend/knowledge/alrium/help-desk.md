# Help desk and support requests

## How to raise a request

Tell CoO (for example, "the printer on floor 2 is broken"), email the right team, or use the help desk portal. Say what's wrong, where, and how urgent it is.

- IT (laptops, accounts, software, network): it-helpdesk@alrium.example
- Facilities (rooms, cleaning, air conditioning, supplies, building access): facilities@alrium.example
- People (leave, letters, benefits): hr@alrium.example
- Finance (expenses, payroll, invoices): finance@alrium.example

## Priorities and response times

- P1, critical (the office has no power or internet, or there's a security incident): first response within 1 hour, fixed within 4 hours.
- P2, high (a whole team can't work, or a meeting room screen fails before a client visit): first response within 4 hours, fixed within 1 working day.
- P3, normal (one person's problem, with a workaround): first response within 1 working day, fixed within 3 working days.
- P4, request (new equipment, access, supplies): first response within 2 working days, done within 5 working days.

## Help desk hours

The help desk works 9:00 to 19:00, Monday to Friday. For P1 issues outside those hours, call the on-call number on the notice board next to each lift. IT and Facilities share the on-call rota.

## Tracking a request

Every request gets a ticket number, for example FAC-1001 for facilities or IT-2001 for IT. You'll get updates by email, and you can reply to that email to add details.
