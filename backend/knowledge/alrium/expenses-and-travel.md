# Expenses and travel

## Claiming expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days, with itemized receipts, through the finance portal. Your manager approves claims up to ₹25,000; larger claims also need the Finance Lead's approval. Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary. Questions go to finance@alrium.example.

## What you can claim

- Travel for work, booked under this policy.
- Meals while travelling: a per diem of ₹2,500 a day in India.
- Client meals: up to ₹3,000 per person, with the client's name on the claim.
- Work equipment and books approved by your manager.
- Home internet: ₹1,000 a month (see "Payroll, benefits and reviews").

You can't claim personal items, fines, upgrades you chose, or anything without a receipt.

## Booking travel

Book through the travel desk (travel@alrium.example) so your trip is covered by Alrium's travel insurance. Domestic flights are economy class. Hotels can cost up to ₹6,000 a night, or ₹8,000 in Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru. Any trip costing more than ₹40,000 in total needs approval before booking. International travel needs approval from your department head and Finance.

## Company cards

Department heads and the Operations Manager hold company cards for vendor payments and team events. Card spending must be matched with receipts in the finance portal by the 5th of the following month.
