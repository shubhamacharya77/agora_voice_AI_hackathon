# Leave and holidays

## Leave year and how to request leave

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Request leave at least 5 working days ahead, except for sick leave and emergencies. You can ask CoO to request leave for you, or use the HR portal. Your manager approves or declines within 2 working days, and approved leave appears in the team calendar automatically.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 20 days of paid annual leave a year. Leave builds up at 1.67 days a month, and new joiners get a pro-rated amount in their first year. You can carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they lapse. Unused leave isn't paid out, except when you leave Alrium (see "Joining and leaving Alrium").

## Sick leave

You get 10 days of paid sick leave a year. Tell your manager as early as you can on the day. For more than 2 sick days in a row, share a medical certificate with the People team. Sick leave doesn't carry over.

## Parental, bereavement and other leave

- Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act.
- Paternity and partner leave: 2 weeks of paid leave, taken within 6 months of a birth or adoption.
- Adoption leave: 12 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver.
- Bereavement leave: 5 days of paid leave for the loss of a close family member.
- Marriage leave: 3 days of paid leave, once.
- Unpaid leave: up to 30 days a year, with your manager's and the People team's approval.

## Public and festival holidays

Alrium has 10 paid holidays a year, plus 2 floating holidays.

Fixed holidays in both offices: New Year's Day (1 January), Republic Day (26 January), May Day (1 May), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October) and Christmas (25 December).

Four festival holidays are announced each January, and they can differ between Bengaluru and Pune, for example Ugadi, Holi, Eid, Diwali or Pongal.

Floating holidays: choose any 2 days a year for festivals or occasions that matter to you, and request them like annual leave.

## Checking your balance

Your leave balance is in the HR portal, and CoO can tell you too ("What's my leave balance?"). Questions about leave go to hr@alrium.example.
