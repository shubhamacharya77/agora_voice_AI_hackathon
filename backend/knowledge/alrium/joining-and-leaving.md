# Joining and leaving Alrium

## Before your first day

After you accept your offer, the People team runs a background check and IT prepares your laptop and accounts. A week before you start, you get a welcome email with your start time, your manager's name and your onboarding buddy.

## Your first weeks

- Day 1: the People team welcomes you at 9:30, then IT sets up your laptop and MFA.
- In your first week, you and your manager agree a 30-60-90 day plan.
- Your buddy helps with tools, people and how things work here.
- Finish the security, data protection and code of conduct training within your first two weeks.

## Probation

Probation lasts 3 months. Your manager checks in every month and confirms your probation at the end of it. During probation, the notice period is 30 days.

## Leaving Alrium

After probation, the notice period is 60 days; your manager and the People team can agree a shorter one. Before your last day:

- Hand over your work and write down anything only you know.
- Return your laptop, ID card and any other equipment to IT and reception.
- Have an exit conversation with the People team.

Your full and final settlement, including pay for unused annual leave, is paid within 45 days of your last day. You get your relieving and experience letters on your last day or soon after.
