# Payroll, benefits and reviews

## Payroll

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. Payslips appear in the HR portal by the 1st of the following month. Submit tax-saving declarations by 30 April and the proofs by 31 January. Payroll questions go to finance@alrium.example.

## Health and insurance

- Group health insurance: a ₹5 lakh family cover for you, your spouse or partner, and up to two children. You can add parents at a subsidized rate during enrolment each April.
- Term life insurance: 3 times your annual fixed pay.
- Personal accident cover: ₹10 lakh.
- A free annual health check-up at partner clinics.

## Allowances and perks

- Learning budget: ₹25,000 a year for courses, books and conferences, with your manager's approval.
- Home office: a one-time ₹10,000 allowance for a desk or chair, plus ₹1,000 a month for internet.
- Wellness: ₹1,500 a month for a gym, sports or therapy.
- Lunch is provided at the Bengaluru office on weekdays; the Pune team gets a meal card.
- Referral bonus: ₹50,000 when someone you refer joins and completes 90 days.

## Performance reviews

Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. You write a short self-review, your manager adds feedback from the people you work with, and together you agree goals for the next six months. Pay changes and promotions take effect on 1 April. You can talk to your manager or the People team (hr@alrium.example) about your growth at any time; you don't need to wait for a review.
